MEET YOUR COACH

Hi, I'm Adrian.

I'm the coach behind Flow State Swimming Academy, passionate about helping swimmers develop confidence, technique, and a genuine love for the water.

My coaching philosophy is simple: meet swimmers where they are, build strong fundamentals, and help them progress with confidence.

MY COACHING PHILOSOPHY

Good coaching is about the swimmer.

Every swimmer has a different starting point. Some need to overcome fear of the water. Others need help refining technique or preparing for more demanding training.

My role is to create a structured environment where swimmers understand what they are doing, why they are doing it, and how they can improve.
